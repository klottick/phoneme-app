## Audio and phoneme preparation

All 15 retained originals are mono, 16 kHz, IEEE float32 WAVs. Each original is copied byte-for-byte to `originals/<item-id>.wav`. Each clip is a full-range `[0, frameCount)` conversion to mono 16 kHz `pcm-s16le` using the published `pcm16-v1` contract; no sample boundary was guessed, and there was no resampling, channel mixing, gain change, normalization, dither, or randomness. The lossy quantization is documented in each provenance entry. Original and clip encodings, dimensions, duration, byte length, and SHA-256 are measured separately.

The retained eSpeak NG 1.52.0 executable and `fr-fr` voice were used only in quiet IPA mode (`-q -v fr-fr --ipa=3 --stdin --path=<isolated pinned data directory>`). All 15 outputs were rerun against the exact text and matched the retained output/input hashes. No audio synthesis or playback was requested. `rawSentenceIpa` preserves the whole-sentence tool output; token phonemes and proposed answer keys are machine-generated, word-aligned drafts. Token phoneme arrays are ordered unique symbol-membership sets (repeated occurrences are collapsed in first-occurrence order); stress/boundary marks are omitted. There are no human corrections and no audio-derived word boundaries. eSpeak output is not a pronunciation reference and cannot establish that the recording says the displayed text.
